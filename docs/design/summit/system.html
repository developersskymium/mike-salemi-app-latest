Mike Salemi App · Direction 3
Design system

Summit

A daylight, photo-led system for training with Mike. Strong type, real colour, and progress you can see — every member is on a climb.

Principles

01
Mike leads
Full-colour, warm photography of Mike on every key surface. Never tinted, never desaturated.
02
Daylight surfaces
Warm chalk ground, crisp white cards, three clear levels of depth, generous space.
03
Bold, legible type
Archivo for confident headlines and numbers, Inter for everything you read.
04
Glacier + Ember
Glacier blue means act. Ember means progress, streaks and achievement. Ink for text.
05
The Climb
Progress is a route up Mount Shasta: phases are camps, levels are summits.
06
One next step
Every screen has one obvious primary action — usually "start" or "resume".
07
Offers as the next camp
Upgrades appear where they help your progress, with real value. Never as ads.
