Typography — three premium pairings

All three are SIL Open Font License 1.1: free for commercial use, self-hosting and bundling in the iOS/Android apps. Each is shown on the same hero, card and table. Fontshare (Satoshi, General Sans, Switzer, Cabinet Grotesk) could not be downloaded here (blocked) and its ITF licence forbids redistributing the files, so it is left out. Geist was tested and dropped: it is the v0/Vercel default and reads as "AI-built".
