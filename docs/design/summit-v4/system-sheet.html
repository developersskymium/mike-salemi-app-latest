Rules
  • Glacier = do this. Primary buttons, links, active nav, the current step. Never decorative.
  • Ember = progress & achievement. Progress bars, streaks, completed checks, the climb line. Never a button.
  • One raised card per view. Everything else rests on a hairline. No shadows on lists or grids.
  • Every card has a head: 6px tick · label · hairline rule · right meta. Footer actions sit in a tray.
  • Photos are mounted (6px frame, radius 14). Tags on photos are white pills — colour lives in data, not chrome.
  • Concentric radii: inner = outer − padding (20 → 14). Spacing on a 4pt grid: 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 40.
  • Numbers in the display face, tabular, tight; units in body size and ink 3.
