# QA review: Mike Salemi App v2 (Summit), branch `ui/qa` (copy of `v2` @ c965788)

Independent review against the client's bar ("peak, high end, very intuitive, lots of Mike's pictures, minimal glass, not washed out, sidebar like the original, smooth not jerky, no card covers with baked-in text, images never cut off"), the approved Summit mockups (`docs/design/summit/shots`), spec 10 (rollout), spec 08 §L (original layouts) and spec 07 (signature per page).

## How this was tested

- `MOCK_DATA=1 pnpm --filter web dev --port 3151`, headless Chrome (puppeteer-core) driven by `docs/qa/capture.mjs`.
- **117 route variants**: every `page.tsx` under `apps/web/src/app`, with dynamic params from `apps/web/fixtures`, plus `?preview=` states for retreats, coaching and organisation (`docs/qa/routes.json`). Each was captured at 1440×900 and 390×844, in light and dark, as viewport and full-page shots: **468 captures**, in `docs/qa/shots/<route>--<viewport>-<theme>[-full].png`. To keep the repo light, the committed PNGs are 256-colour, and phone shots are stored at 1× (390 px wide). Re-run `node docs/qa/capture.mjs` for full-fidelity 2× captures.
- Automated checks per capture (`docs/qa/results.json`): console errors, failed requests, horizontal overflow, images with `naturalWidth 0`, CLS, first paint, and heavy `object-fit: cover` crops.
- Flows were clicked through with `docs/qa/flow.mjs`. Their shots are in `docs/qa/shots/flows/`:
  - day: gate → readiness (5 answers) → submit → mark steps → workout table → Workout mode → completion → Day complete
  - Self-Guided wizard: Level → Days → Start → Focus → Preview → Save
  - course player: open group → lesson → Next lesson
  - retreat: Apply (4 steps → review → send), then the Schedule-a-call step (pick a date and time → book)
  - coaching: package → date → time → checkout
  - library: Filters → level filter → inline player → movement index
  - shop: size → qty → Add to cart → cart drawer
  - plan: drag and drop on desktop (synthetic HTML5 DnD)
  - theme toggle
  - the phone More sheet

**Environment caveats (not counted as defects).**
- The sandbox cannot reach Vimeo (`i.vimeocdn.com`, `player.vimeo.com`) or `app.mikesalemi.com`. This explains the `_next/image` 500s on `/library`, `/you/saves` and the A4L lesson, the 4 broken Vimeo posters (`/programs/kettlebell-lifestyle?mode=self-guided`, `/session/average-day`, `/studio/program`, `/studio/site`) and the empty video frames.
- Preview writes ("Saving isn't available in the local preview") are expected.
- First-paint numbers are dev-mode (median 864 ms, worst 8.2 s on `/shop/retreat-deposit` on a cold compile), so they are not production numbers.
- No horizontal overflow was found on any route (`scrollWidth == innerWidth` everywhere).
- `/studio/design` at 390 px hangs headless Chrome: its embedded mockup iframes wait on Google Fonts. Its phone shots were skipped.

Severity: **P0** broken · **P1** clearly off-brand, confusing or against an explicit client rule · **P2** polish.
